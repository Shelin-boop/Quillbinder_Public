# Mind Map Help

A mind map is a board for ideas: notes anywhere, joined by lines and arrows. Every document and folder of the project can have one (the **Mind Map** button of the inspector, or **Open in Window** for a large board). **Open Example Board** below opens a practice board that is not part of your project: try everything there. *Menu* means the **Menu** button above the board in the inspector; the large window has the same menus in its menu bar.

---

## Notes

![A note and a stack of notes](qbmm:notes)

- **Double-click** an empty place: a new note, ready for typing. Click outside it, or press **Esc**, when you are done.
- **Double-click** a note to change its text.
- **Ctrl+Enter** makes a new note under the selected one (a stack).
- **Drag** a note to move it. Hold **Ctrl** when you let go, and it is copied.
- **Delete** removes the selected notes.

## Lines: drag one note onto another

![Dragging one note onto another](qbmm:drag)

Take a note with the mouse, drag it onto another note and let go: the two are joined by a line, and the dragged note jumps back to where it was. Drag it onto the same note again and the line goes away.

![Two notes joined by a line](qbmm:line)

## Arrows: hold a key while you let go

| Hold while you let go | You get |
|-----------------------|---------|
| (nothing) | a line, or no line if there was one |
| **Alt** | an arrow pointing to the note you dropped on |
| **Ctrl+Alt** | an arrow pointing back to the note you dragged |
| **Ctrl+Alt+Shift** | arrows at both ends |

![An arrow to the note dropped on](qbmm:arrow)

![An arrow back to the dragged note](qbmm:arrowback)

![Arrows at both ends](qbmm:both)

The same with the keyboard: select the notes (the first one you click is the center), then **Menu > Notes > Connect** (**Ctrl+.**) for lines, **Connect With Arrow** (**Ctrl+Alt+.**) for arrows from the first note to the others, **Remove Arrows** (**Ctrl+,**), and **Disconnect** (**Ctrl+Alt+Shift+.**).

A new note joined at once: select a note and press **Alt** with an arrow key (a line) or **Alt+Shift** with an arrow key (an arrow); or hold **Ctrl** (line) or **Alt** (arrow) while you double-click the empty place.

## The arrows of one line: right-click the line

Right-click a line itself (not a note) for a menu that changes only that line, even when its notes have other lines: **No Arrows**, an arrow to either note, **Arrows at Both Ends**, **Line Color**, and **Icon in the Middle** (an icon drawn halfway along the line).

## Colors, shapes, and icons: right-click a note

Right-click a note for its **Fill Color**, **Text Color**, and **Border Color** (the colors of your labels, the six colors of the format bar, or **Other...**), its **Shape** (Rectangle, Rounded Rectangle, Cloud, Jagged), and **Icons**: up to eight, one at each corner and one in the middle of each side, from the icons of the binder.

## Words on a line

![Words on a line](qbmm:label)

Select the two notes and choose **Menu > Notes > Connection Label…** (**Ctrl+Shift+L**). Double-click the label to change it. A double-click on a line without a label puts a new note into the middle of the line.

## Background shapes

![Notes on a background shape](qbmm:shape)

**Menu > Notes > New Background Shape** (**Ctrl+Shift+G**) puts a colored area behind the selected notes. A magnetic shape (the default) takes the notes on it along when you move it.

## Looking around

- **Ctrl+mouse wheel** zooms; hold **Space** and drag to move the board; hold **Z** to see the whole board for a moment.
- **Ctrl+F** finds text in the notes.
- **Ctrl+Z** undoes the last change of the board (while the board has the keyboard).

The **Format** menu colors notes and shapes, changes borders and shapes, and has the **Icons** as well; **Note Styles** keep a look under a name. Everything else is in the manual (**Help > User Manual**, section 7, "Mind maps").
