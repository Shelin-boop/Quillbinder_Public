**Window > Themes** changes the look of the whole window at once, without a restart: Dark Mode, Midnight, Sepia, Slate, Ocean, High Contrast, Light, or Default, which follows your desktop.
**Try it:** choose **Window > Themes > Dark Mode**, look around, and go back to the theme you like.
- **File > Options > Appearance** changes the colors and fonts of each part of the window on top of the theme; **Save Current Look as Theme…** keeps them under a name of your own.
- The **text color** and **highlight** buttons of the format bar: rest the mouse on one for a moment, and a small palette appears (red, blue, yellow, green, white, black, and any other color).
- **Navigate > Fonts > Install Font Files…** adds fonts (TTF, OTF, WOFF, or a ZIP of them) and shows them in the font lists at once.
