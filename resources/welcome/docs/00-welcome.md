This small project shows what {{APP}} has that you may not know from other writing programs. Each document in the folder **Feature Tour** takes a minute or two: read it, try what it says, then go on with the next one.
Nothing you do here touches your own projects. Delete this project whenever you like; **Help > Welcome Project** makes a fresh copy.
Where the settings are, and a summary of everything the program can do: **Help > Quick Start Guide**. Every function in detail: **Help > User Manual** (**F1**).

**The tour**
- [The Icon Bar](qb-title:The Icon Bar): document icons with one click
- [Zoom Where You Point](qb-title:Zoom Where You Point): every part of the window keeps its own zoom
- [Mind Maps](qb-title:Mind Maps): a board of notes and arrows for every document
- [Object Sheets](qb-title:Object Sheets): characters, places, and things, linked to each other
- [Comments of a Whole Folder](qb-title:Comments of a Whole Folder): all notes of a chapter in one list
- [Compare and Transfer](qb-title:Compare and Transfer): a manuscript back from Word or LibreOffice
- [Scrivener Projects](qb-title:Scrivener Projects): import them, or work in them directly
- [Themes, Colors, and Fonts](qb-title:Themes, Colors, and Fonts): dark mode and more, at once
- [Updates and Add-ons](qb-title:Updates and Add-ons): small packages, and a way back
- [Safety Nets](qb-title:Safety Nets): what protects your text
- [Where Next](qb-title:Where Next)
A click on a title opens it. You can also click the documents in the binder on the left.
