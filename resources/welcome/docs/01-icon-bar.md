The **icon bar** holds ten document icons. One click gives an icon to the documents selected in the binder, or in the corkboard or outliner you are working in. The **↺** button after the icons gives them their standard icon back.
**Try it:** in the binder, select two or three documents of the Feature Tour (**Ctrl+click**) and click the green book on the icon bar. Then click **↺**.
- If you do not see the bar: **View > Toolbars > Icon Bar**.
- Choose your own ten icons: **View > Toolbars > Choose Icons for the Icon Bar…**, or right-click the bar. Your own pictures can be on it too.
- **Documents > Change Icon** has 76 icons in groups (books, flags, notepads, characters, locations ...), **Icon from Text…** (up to three letters in a colored box, for example "Ch1"), and **Manage Icons…** for pictures of your own.
- With **View > Use Label Color In > Icons**, the white parts of an icon take the color of the document's label.
