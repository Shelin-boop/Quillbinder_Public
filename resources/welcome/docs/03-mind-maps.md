Every document and folder can have a **mind map**: a board of notes, lines, arrows, colors, and shapes. This document has one.
**Try it:** click the **Mind Map** button of the inspector, between Bookmarks and Metadata (the button is underlined when the document has a board). The board on it shows what mind maps can do; **Zoom to Fit** above the board shows all of it. **Open in Window** above the board shows the same board in a large window with an inspector of its own.
- **Double-click** an empty place for a new note. Drag a note onto another to draw a line; hold **Alt** while you let go for an arrow.
- Right-click a note for its colors, shape, border, and icons; right-click a line for its arrows, its color, and an icon in the middle.
- **Menu > Notes > Add Notes to the Binder** turns notes into documents of this project.
- A board can be exported as PDF, PNG, text, OPML, or a Scapple file, and Scapple files can be read into a board.
- **Help > Mind Map Help** explains it with pictures and has a practice board.
