The fog came down from [Fenwood](qbobj-name:Fenwood) before noon, the way it did every year once the birches had turned.
[Mara Fenn](qbobj-name:Mara Fenn) tied the ferry to the last post and listened. Somewhere behind the reeds, [Tobias Quill](qbobj-name:Tobias Quill) was calling her name, too loud, as if [Ashvale](qbobj-name:Ashvale) were a city and not forty houses along a slow river.
"You're late," she said when he came out of the [fog](qbobj-name:Autumn Fog).
"I found the old ferry book," he said. "Your mother's name is in it. Twice."
