Hold **Ctrl** and turn the mouse wheel over any part of the window: only that part grows or shrinks. The keyboard can stay where it is.
**Try it:** make the binder a little larger, then the synopsis card in the inspector, then this text. Each part keeps its own size, also after a restart.
- The inspector has a zoom for each of its parts: synopsis and notes, the bookmark lists and the bookmarked text, the snapshots, the objects. The **Zoom** list at the bottom of the inspector names the part you zoomed last.
- The corkboard (cards and their text together), the outliner, mind maps, composition mode, and Quick Reference windows zoom the same way.
- **Ctrl+0** gives the editor its standard zoom back. The start value of every part is under **File > Options > Editing** ("Default text zoom").
- {{APP}} also remembers where you were: every document opens with the cursor where you left it, and pictures and PDF files keep their zoom and page.
