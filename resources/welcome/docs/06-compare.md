A manuscript often comes back from an editor as a Word or LibreOffice file, or you go on working on it there for a while. **File > Compare and Transfer > Compare with File…** shows what differs between such a file and your project, and takes over the side you choose.
- **Update the project from the file**: the changes go into your documents. Every changed document gets a snapshot first, so you can always go back.
- **Update the file from the project**: {{APP}} writes a copy of the file beside it. In a Word copy, every change is a tracked change that you accept or reject in Word or LibreOffice.
- **By Chapter** lists the documents with their changes, each with a tick box; **Step by Step** shows one change at a time.
- Green text comes in, red struck-through text goes, blue text gets the other side's bold, italic, or underline.
The file is compared with what **Compile** makes of the project, so compile once and keep the file you send out.
