Test files of tests/m11_tests.cpp and tests/v050_tests.cpp (audio and video)

All five files were made for these tests with FFmpeg (encoder tag Lavf 60.16.100): a plain
color picture and a generated tone, about 10 s each. They contain no recording, music or
picture of anyone else.

red.mp4      H.264 video, red, with an AAC tone
blue.webm    VP9 video, blue, with an Opus tone
green.mkv    MPEG-4 video, green, in Matroska
tone.mp3     MP3 tone with invented tags (title "Harbour Interview", artist "Old Keeper")
tone.ogg     Ogg Vorbis tone

Like the rest of Quillbinder's own files, they are under the Zero-Clause BSD license (LICENSE).
